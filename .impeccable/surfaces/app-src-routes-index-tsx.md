# Surface brief: drhoodskin.com home

Scope: the single public page (`app/src/routes/index.tsx`, `app/src/styles.css`). Mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: Golden / west Denver patients, mostly on phones, deciding on Dr. Hood and requesting a visit. Existing patients need phone, hours, directions, forms.
Action: request an appointment with Dr. Hood at USDP Golden, or call. Every path says what it does (request and callback vs. booked time vs. phone).
Proof: headshot, training and distinctions, TIME, JAAD International, two dated Google reviews, external profiles.
Constraints: no PAs; services, procedures, and conditions content stays unchanged (owner decision); sourced claims only; no promised availability.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A premium dermatology practice page for one physician: calm porcelain and stone neutrals, a gallery-framed portrait, credentials hung as quiet gallery frames, and a booking card that says exactly what each path does. Refuses both the generic medical template (teal, icon cards, stock photos) and the first round's green-and-brass costume.

OWN-WORLD: Porcelain paper ground with warm stone bands; white cards; slate ink; one deep blue-slate action color; thin near-black gallery frames with white mats; Marcellus in mixed case for her name and section headings; Source Sans 3 for all reading and controls; the portrait's sky blue appears only in the photograph.

STORY: The visitor meets Dr. Hood's face and name, sees how to book and what happens next, reads what patients say, then her training, finds the office and live hours and how to prepare, and leaves with the booking card. The parked services section sits after the practical sections.
