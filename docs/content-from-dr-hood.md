# What only Dr. Hood can provide

These are the highest-value additions to drhoodskin.com that need her words, permission, or photos. Nothing here will be written for her or guessed. Short answers are fine; they can be edited together.

1. **"My approach" in her own voice (3–4 sentences, first person).** The best physician pages (NYU Langone, UnionDerm) lead with this. Prompts: What do you enjoy most about dermatology? How do you like to work with patients on a plan? What should a new patient expect at a first visit? (Patients already praise her for explaining several options and involving them in decisions.)
2. **One personal line.** Where she grew up, or something she enjoys outside work. Patients choose people.
3. **Permission to show her own Google rating and review count** (her individual profile only, with the date). The site currently shows two quotes and no totals.
4. **Languages she speaks**, if any besides English.
5. **A real photo of the Golden office or building entrance**, plus parking and entrance details (which door, free parking?). Real photos beat stock photos for trust.
6. **Optional: a 30–60 second phone video** introducing herself.
7. **Two confirmations:** she still sees children as well as adults; and whether the parked services section can stay as it is (it lists Botox, which she offers, and the older wording).
